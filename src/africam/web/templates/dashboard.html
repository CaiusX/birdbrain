BirdBrain pulls the audio from Africam and explore.org live cams across Southern and East Africa, runs BirdNET on rolling three-second windows, and logs what it hears.

Each dot on the map is a camera. The threads between them weigh species heard at both sites over the last 30 days — a rough read on which cameras share their soundscape. Click a dot for that site's 24-hour activity clock and most-recently-heard species.
